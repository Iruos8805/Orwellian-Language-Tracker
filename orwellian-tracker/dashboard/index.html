◄ METHODOLOGY

■ DATA PIPELINE OVERVIEW

LEXICAL MODULE: Uses MATTR (Moving-Average Type-Token Ratio) with a 50-token sliding window. Diversity Loss = 1 – MATTR. Source: political speech corpora tokenised via spaCy.

SYNTACTIC MODULE: AMR (Abstract Meaning Representation) parse depth measures sentence structural complexity. Logic_flat captures proportion of simple paratactic constructions vs complex hypotaxis.

SEMANTIC MODULE: Word2vec embeddings trained per-decade. Cosine drift between decade pairs measures meaning shift. Drift Rate = mean cosine distance of top-1000 political terms from prior decade centroid.

NEWSPEAK INDEX: Normalised composite: 0.4·(Diversity Loss) + 0.3·(Logic_flat) + 0.3·(Drift Rate). Score range: 0 (natural language) → 1 (fully controlled Newspeak).
